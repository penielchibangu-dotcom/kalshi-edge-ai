How to use it

  1. Find a market on Kalshi and enter its contract price.
  2. Estimate the true probability independently; do not simply copy the market price.
  3. Compare your estimate with the price and consider fees, liquidity, and the possibility of losing the full stake.
  4. Track ideas here as paper positions before risking real money.

This page does not connect to your Kalshi account, fetch live quotes, provide guaranteed picks, or place trades. Paper positions are saved only in this browser on this device.
